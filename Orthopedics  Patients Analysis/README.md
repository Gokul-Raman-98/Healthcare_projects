## Project Description
Patients analysis of orthopedics department for the year 2023.

1. KPI section includes count of patients, average satisfaction score and average patient waittime.
2. Table view includes the no.of patients and average satisfaction score for each month for the year 2023.
3. Chart view includes the no. of patients with respect to age groups and the no.of patients and their waittime in different timestamp.
